# CloakForge: Market Research and Monetization

**How to read this report.** Sourced facts carry a source tag such as (S6) that points to the Sources list. **Est.** marks my own estimates, with the assumptions stated. All USD figures use SOL = $103.85 (CoinGecko, 2026-10-16) unless a source gives USD itself (S30). Data was gathered on 2026-10-16. The Ahrefs and Similarweb connectors failed to connect for this session, so traffic and keyword figures come from public pages only.

## 1. Verdict

**Launch, but treat it as a lean, SEO-led side business, not a primary income. Base case is about $19k in year 1 (Est.).** The no-code token-tool niche is real but shrinking. The category leader Smithii's on-chain revenue fell 91% from January 2025 to September 2026 (S6), and free launchpads now take almost all meme launches. Launch only if these conditions are met:

1. Fix the legal blockers in [04-legal-compliance](./04-legal-compliance.mdx) and switch to mainnet.
2. Make the mock auditor real, or hide it.
3. Ship the "safe launch" bundle (section 6) so you compete on trust, not only on price.
4. Set a day-90 kill/keep threshold (section 8).

**Is "create token + add LP manually" demand shrinking?** Yes. It is still meaningful only for non-meme use: utility, DAO, RWA or community tokens, airdrops, tax tokens and teams that need to keep mint control.

- **Sourced.** Smithii's revenue fell faster than pump.fun's (down 91% vs 78%, Jan 2025 to Sep 2026) (S6).
- **Sourced.** The newcomer tool site Solauncher earns $400–830/month (Apr–Sep 2026) (S6).
- **Est.** All no-code tool sites combined do about 50–200 paid manual creations per day. That is under 0.5% of launchpad launches.

**Is about 0.1 SOL competitive?** Yes. 0.1 SOL is about $10.4 or €9.3 (2026-10-16) (S30), which is at or below the median for every action. But price cannot be the edge:

- Orion (0.03) and FluxBeam (free) undercut it.
- The rent cut (S29) lowers everyone's floor.
- Users punish per-revoke upsells and "hidden" fees (S12, S13).

## 5. Revenue scenarios (year 1)

**Market sizing (all Est.)**

- **Launch economy:** over $400M/yr, mostly launchpad trading fees (pump.fun $324M over 12 months) (S6). CloakForge cannot address this.
- **Addressable market, top-down:** paid no-code tool sites on Solana take about $1–3M/yr. This assumes Smithii ($469k over 12 months) has a 15–35% share.
- **Bottom-up check:** 50–200 paid manual launches per day × about 0.3 SOL each × $103.85 × 365 gives about $0.57–2.27M/yr. This agrees with the top-down figure.

| Scenario | Assumptions | Year-1 revenue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conservative | 1.5 paid actions/day on average at 0.1 SOL. SEO slow to rank. About the Solauncher tier (S6) | about 55 SOL, **about $5.7k** |
| **Base** | Ramps from about 1/day (months 1–3) to 10+/day (months 10–12), averaging 5/day. Safe-launch bundle lifts spend per customer. About 1–3% of the addressable market | about 183 SOL, **about $19k** |
| Optimistic | 20 actions/day on average. Affiliate channel works and the meme season (Oct 2026, S3) holds. About 16% of Smithii's 12-month revenue | about 730 SOL, **about $76k** |

**Sensitivities:**

- Revenue is earned in SOL. At $150/SOL every figure rises by about 45%.
- Running costs (RPC, IPFS pinning, hosting, accountant) are an estimated $2–4k/yr. The conservative case barely breaks even.

Deprioritize the following:

- **OpenBook market cost reduction:** obsolete, since AMM v4 dropped OpenBook (S2).
- **Multi-chain:** tool demand is moving to Robinhood Chain (S26), but Smithii and CoinFactory are already there, and the effort is high.
- **Telegram bot:** signing on Telegram conflicts with the non-custodial model.
- **White-label / API:** no demand evidence found.
- **Bundlers and volume bots:** never build these. They risk market manipulation and damage the brand.

## 8. 90-day go-to-market plan

**Days 0–14: launch hygiene**

- Fix report 04 blockers and switch to mainnet.
- Fix the multisender zero-fee bug from report 05.
- Apply the new prices from section 4.
- Add cookieless analytics and a public Dune query on the fee wallet.

**Days 0–14: free listings**

- MadeOnSol (it has "Submit a Tool"), Solana Compass, DappRadar, Alchemy Dapp Store.
- A **DefiLlama fees adapter PR**: Smithii and Solauncher are both listed there (S6). A listing is free and builds credibility.
- Product Hunt for one backlink.

**Days 15–45: content.** Write 12–15 long-tail pages:

- revoke mint/freeze authority
- cheapest solana multisender
- solana token cost 2026
- token-2022 transfer fee token
- burn LP raydium cpmm
- freeze authority before raydium
- honest "vs Smithii / vs Orion" pages

Google autocomplete shows price-sensitive intent ("free", "cheap", "no code", "cheapest solana multisender", 2026-10-16) (S33). Validate search volumes in Google Keyword Planner first; no public volume data was retrievable. Add 3–5 YouTube tutorials, because autocomplete shows "create solana token youtube" (S33).

**Days 15–45: launch the real auditor** as a free page that can be shared on X.

**Days 30–90: distribution**

- Run an X account with daily "trust check" threads on new tokens.
- Open a Telegram support group.
- Launch the affiliate program at 20–30% revenue share.
- Contact 20–30 launch agencies, KOL "dev" accounts and Telegram group admins.

**Day 90: keep / kill gate (Est. thresholds).**

- **Keep investing:** at least 30 paid actions/week, or at least 1,500 organic visits/month with rising rankings.
- **Below 10 paid actions/week:** freeze development and run it as a low-maintenance site.
