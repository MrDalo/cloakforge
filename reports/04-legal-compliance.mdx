# CloakForge: Legal and Regulatory Compliance Review

## 1. Disclaimer

This is not legal advice. It is a practical gap analysis based on the repository and the live site (fetched read-only on 2026-10-16). Have a Czech advokát, and a daňový poradce for VAT and income tax, review it before launch.

## 2. Verdict

**Not launch-ready from a legal standpoint, but fixable in about 1–2 dev days plus a lawyer review.** The core positioning holds: a non-custodial builder of unsigned transactions is very likely not a MiCA CASP, not an AML obliged entity and not a US money services business (MSB). The blockers are elsewhere:

- The **live** imprint, terms and privacy policy still show `[YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]`-style placeholders.
- Fee-paying consumers never click to accept the terms, and nothing validly waives their right of withdrawal.
- The privacy and marketing copy makes provably false claims ("zero data collection", "No IP logging", "complete anonymity"), while server code runs in the US (`iad1`).
- There is no sanctions geo-blocking or wallet screening.

## 5. Must-fix before launch

**1. Operator identity on all three legal pages.** Legal basis: §435 Civil Code, ECD Art. 5, GDPR Art. 13(1)(a), CRD Art. 6(1)(b)–(c). Files: `imprintData.ts`, the `contact` section of `termsData.ts`, and the `controller` section of `privacyData.ts`.

> "Operator: [Name Surname], sole trader (OSVČ) trading as CloakForge. Place of business: [street, postcode, city], Czech Republic. IČO: [12345678]. Registered in the Trade Register (živnostenský rejstřík) kept by [Obecní živnostenský úřad X]. VAT: not a VAT payer (neplátce DPH) / DIČ CZ…. Email: help@cloakforge.app."

Tip: a virtual-office address registered as your místo podnikání keeps your home address off the site.

**2. Clickwrap acceptance and withdrawal waiver before every paid signature.** Legal basis: CRD Art. 16(a), 16(m) and 8(7); §1837 a) and l) and §1826 Civil Code. Add an unticked required checkbox and disable the Sign button until it is ticked:

> "☐ I agree to the Terms of Service (version 2026-10-xx) and have read the Privacy Policy. I expressly request that CloakForge perform the service immediately and acknowledge that I lose my right of withdrawal once the transaction is confirmed on-chain."

After confirmation, offer an auto-download confirmation file (PDF or HTML) on a durable medium. It should contain the service, fee, tx signature, date, the consent wording above, and the **full text** of the terms version. A hyperlink alone does not count (CJEU C-49/11 *Content Services*).

Without this step, consumers keep the 14-day withdrawal right. If they were never properly informed, the right extends by up to 12 months (CRD Art. 10), and they owe nothing for performance during that period (Art. 14(4)). In practice, every fee becomes refundable on request.

**3. A correct and complete privacy policy.** Legal basis: GDPR Art. 5, 13, 14, 44 ff. At minimum, add a controller block, a purpose → legal basis → retention table, named processors with country and transfer mechanism, the on-chain memo, IPFS permanence, the ÚOOÚ, and an Art. 21 notice. See section 7.

**4. Remove false or unsubstantiated claims.** Legal basis: GDPR Art. 5(1)(a), UCPD Art. 6, §4–5 Act 634/1992, FTC Act §5.

| Claim (where) | Replace with |

Wallet activity is public on-chain." |

**5. Sanctions controls.** Legal basis: Reg 833/2014 Art. 5b(2), Reg 269/2014, Reg 765/2006, Reg 2022/263, and OFAC. The US nexus comes through US providers Vercel and Alchemy.

- **Geo-block** in a Next.js proxy/middleware using `x-vercel-ip-country` and `x-vercel-ip-country-region`:
  - Countries: CU, IR, KP, RU, BY.
  - Ukrainian regions: UA-43 (Crimea), UA-40 (Sevastopol), UA-14 (Donetsk), UA-09 (Luhansk). For the EU occupied-areas regime, conservatively also UA-23 (Zaporizhzhia) and UA-65 (Kherson).
  - Syria: OFAC terminated the Syria program on 1 Jul 2025, so blocking is optional.
- **Wallet screening:** before building any transaction, check the connected wallet, multisender recipients and freeze targets against the OFAC SDN digital-currency addresses (free list), or use a free sanctions-screening API.
- **Terms change:** replace the clause "We rely on user representations and are not obligated to monitor, geolocate…" with:

> "We use IP-based geo-blocking and screen wallet addresses against public sanctions lists and will refuse to build transactions for blocked regions or listed addresses. Circumventing these controls (e.g., by VPN) is a material breach of these Terms."

**6. DSA basics for IPFS uploads.** Legal basis: DSA Art. 11, 12, 14, 16, 17, 18. CloakForge stores user content on its own Pinata account and serves it publicly, which makes it a hosting provider. Add to the terms and the imprint:

> "Points of contact (DSA Art. 11–12): legal@cloakforge.app; languages: English, Czech. To report illegal content (Art. 16), email abuse@cloakforge.app with the IPFS CID or URL, why it is illegal, your name and email, and a good-faith statement. We unpin reported content from our Pinata account and block it on our gateway; copies pinned by third parties on IPFS are outside our control."

> "Content rules: do not upload content that infringes others' IP (e.g., third-party logos), impersonates brands or persons, contains other people's personal data, or is otherwise illegal. We may unpin such content and, where we have your contact details, will tell you why (Art. 17)."

**7. Complaints and ADR.** Legal basis: §13 and §14 Act 634/1992; §2389a ff. Civil Code.

> "Complaints: if the Service did not build the transaction you configured, email help@cloakforge.app within two years with the transaction signature. We decide within 30 days. Out-of-court consumer disputes: Czech Trade Inspection Authority (ČOI), Štěpánská 567/15, 120 00 Praha 2, adr.coi.cz."
